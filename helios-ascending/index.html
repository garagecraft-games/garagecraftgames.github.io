About​

Helios Ascending is a twin-stick shooter that originated as a university project at Trier University of Applied Sciences during the Winter Semester 2025/26.

It is built on top of helios — a custom C++23 game engine developed from first principles, emphasizing modern software engineering, ECS architecture, and a hand-rolled OpenGL rendering pipeline.
